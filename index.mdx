Appearance ID is what you look like — captured once, usable anywhere.

Users complete a face scan. When they connect their Appearance ID to your service, you receive reference photos of them. Your agent uses those photos to generate images of that person.

## What you receive

Multi-angle reference photos of the user from their most recent scan.

## How it works

1. Your agent connects to the Appearance MCP server
2. The user authenticates and approves sharing
3. Your agent calls `get_appearance` and receives their photos
4. Generate images using your own models with those photos as reference
5. Call `save_generation` to store the result in the user's profile

Results from every connected service appear in one place.

## What are Zones?

An Appearance Zone is an independent vertical of human appearance. Each zone captures a different aspect of how you look.

| Zone | Status |
|------|--------|
| **Hair** | Live |
| **Glasses** | Live |
| **Nails** | Planned |
| **Tattoos** | Planned |

## Zones in the API

When calling `save_generation` or `search_styles`, pass the `zone` parameter to scope to a specific zone.

New zones will be announced as they launch.

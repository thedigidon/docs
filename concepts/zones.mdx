## What are Zones?

An Appearance Zone is an independent vertical of human appearance. Each zone has its own data model, business types, and matching logic.

```typescript
type AppearanceZone = 'hair' | 'glasses' | 'nails' | 'tattoos'
```

## Zone Registry

| Zone | Status | Business Types | Model |
|------|--------|---------------|-------|
| **Hair** | Live | Barbershop, Salon | Style (provider uploads work, clients try on) |
| **Glasses** | Live | Optician | Product (catalog-based, measurement fitting) |
| **Nails** | Planned | Nail Studio | Style |
| **Tattoos** | Planned | Tattoo Parlor | Style |

## Business Types

Each zone is served by one or more business types. Business types determine the UX copy, categories, and matching approach.

| Business Type | Zone | Categories |
|--------------|------|------------|
| `barbershop` | Hair | Fade, Taper, Textured, Classic, Creative, Buzz, Long |
| `salon` | Hair | Bob, Layers, Balayage, Blowout, Updo, Pixie, Long |
| `optician` | Glasses | Rectangular, Round, Aviator, Cat Eye, Square, Oval |
| `nail_studio` | Nails | Coming soon |
| `tattoo_parlor` | Tattoos | Coming soon |

## Business Models

Two models determine how offerings work:

| Model | Description | Business Types |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Style** | Provider uploads photos of their work. Users try them on with AI generation. | Barbershop, Salon, Nail Studio, Tattoo Parlor |
| **Product** | Business has a product catalog. Users try on individual products. | Optician |

## Zone-Aware Matching

Each zone uses different matching strategies:

**Hair** — Card-based structured matching (texture, length, density, face shape) at 90% weight + embedding aesthetic similarity at 10%.

**Glasses** — Measurement-based fitting (pupillary distance, face width, bridge width) at 60% weight + face shape compatibility at 25% + embedding similarity at 15%.

**Nails / Tattoos** — Planned. Expected to lean heavily on aesthetic matching (embedding similarity) since physical compatibility constraints are minimal.

## Zone in API Responses

Every Appearance Card, Profile, and Recommendation includes a `zone` field. When consuming the API, filter by zone to get relevant data:

```json
// User's hair card
{
  "cardType": "user",
  "zone": "hair",
  "textureFamily": 3,
  "faceShape": "oval"
}

// User's glasses profile
{
  "zone": "glasses",
  "binocularPdMm": 64,
  "frameSize": "medium"
}
```

Zone-specific fields are `null` when they don't apply. For example, `textureFamily` is always `null` on a glasses card.
